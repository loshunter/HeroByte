Quick starts

Just enough to get through your first session.

PLAYER QUICK START

  1. 1Open the invite link from your DM and type the table password.
  2. 2Find your token on the map. Your character is in the Party along the bottom.
  3. 3Drag your token to move it.
  4. 4Open Dice, pick your dice and roll.
Read the player quick start

DM QUICK START

  1. 1Create a private table, then enter the DM password to become the DM.
  2. 2Put a map on the table: upload art, build a room, or generate one.
  3. 3Invite players: copy the link, and send the table password separately.
  4. 4Turn on Player View to check what they will see.

Quick fixes

The things that most often stop a session.

  • Wrong passwordPasswords are case-sensitive. What you typed stays in the field so you can spot the typo.
  • Held in another windowYour seat is open on another device. Keep playing there, or close it there first.
  • Open in another tabEach table runs in one tab per browser. Press RECLAIM THIS TAB to switch back.
  • Everything is darkYou see from your own tokens. Move one, or ask your DM about your sight radius.
  • Voice isn't workingPress Join voice and allow the microphone. Hearing nobody? Press Tap to hear voice if it shows. On mobile data, try Wi-Fi.
  • Still saving the last changeNothing was half-applied. Wait for saving to clear, then draw it again.
  • I lost my tablePaste its table code on the join screen and press JOIN.

Words to know

A few terms first

TABLE
One shared game space with its own map, tokens, dice log and passwords. What each player can see depends on their characters and the fog.
DM
The player who entered the DM password. Gets the DM Menu, the map editor, fog and the player lens.
MAIN HALL
The public test table. Build freely, but it clears itself after an hour empty.
PLAYER VIEW
A DM switch that shows the map exactly as the players see it.
LIVE MAP
A HeroByte map with walls and doors. Fog of war and the Kicked-In Door need one. An uploaded picture becomes one when you draw walls on it.
