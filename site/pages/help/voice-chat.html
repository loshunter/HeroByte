EVERYONECOMPUTER AND PHONE

Voice chat

Like joining a Discord call: one press to join, and you hear everyone in it. It works in the browser on computers and phones, with nothing to install.

Join the call

Press Join voice. Your browser asks for the microphone the first time: allow it. You can see whether a call is running before you join, and how many are in it, for example 2 in call.

On a computerIn the header, in the Panels & settings row. It is always there.
On a phoneAt the top of PARTY, or on the voice chip at the top of the map while a call is running.

Mute, unmute and leave

  • Mute silences your mic and keeps you in the call, so you still hear everyone. Unmute puts you back on.
  • On a computer, the mic button on your character card does the same once you are in.
  • Leave voice hangs up.

See who is talking

  • On a computer, a headphones mark on a character's card means that player is in the call, and a crossed-out speaker means they are muted. Select a row in the Party to see a card, or press CARDS to show them all.
  • When someone talks, their portrait glows green and grows a little.
  • On a phone, the top of PARTY lists everyone in the call by name, with (muted) beside anyone muted.

It comes back by itself

After a network blip, or a reload within a minute, you are back in the call, muted or not, the way you left it. Some browsers ask for the microphone again first. Closing the tab, or a browser restoring it later, never switches your mic on: you press Join voice again.

When it doesn't work

The browser never asked for the mic
Voice needs a secure address, one starting https. Check the address bar, then press Join voice again. If you blocked the mic before, allow it in the browser's site settings.
I joined but can't hear anyone
Some browsers hold the sound until you tap; iPhones are expected to. Press Tap to hear voice when it appears, or tap anywhere on the page.
Someone shows Can't reach
Your two browsers could not connect directly. HeroByte keeps trying. Phones on mobile data often can't: Wi-Fi works best. Someone whose connection dropped without leaving the call can also show this for a few minutes.
It dropped me and says my mic stopped
The mic stopped, for example it was unplugged. Press Join voice to come back. If others can't hear you but you still look live, press Leave voice, then Join voice.
On my phone, the others stopped hearing me
Locking the screen or switching apps can cut a phone's mic. No web page can change that. Press Leave voice, then Join voice.
There's an echo
Someone is on speakers. Headphones fix it.

Voice goes straight between players' browsers. No server records or relays it, and that also means players in the call can see each other's internet (IP) address. Headphones are strongly recommended, so the table doesn't hear itself echo.
