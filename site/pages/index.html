What's on the table

The tools to run your table

BUILD THE MAP LIVE

Drag out rooms, hallways, walls and doors. Each edit lands the moment you let go, and players see it as their characters reach it.

GENERATE A DUNGEON

Party kicked in a door you never prepped? Generate the place behind it in seconds, then edit any part of it.

FOG AND SIGHT

Vision comes from each player's own tokens and stops at walls and closed doors. Secret doors stay hidden until you reveal them.

TORCHLIGHT AND NIGHT

Place torches and braziers, then turn it to night. The map darkens and the light pools glow.

DICE AND COMBAT

Track initiative and turns. Digital rolls come from the server, so nobody fudges. Rolling real dice? Enter them, and they're marked as entered by hand.

A WORLD TO TRAVEL

Link your maps into one campaign and move the whole party between them from the world map.

Before you start

Common questions

Do players need an account?
No. There are no accounts. Players open the invite link and type the table password your DM gives them.
What does it cost?
Nothing, for now. HeroByte is free to use, with nothing to sign up for.
Which games does it work with?
Any tabletop RPG. Maps, tokens, dice and fog work the same whatever you play. Measuring follows the rule you pick for the table: 5e, Pathfinder or straight-line.
Is my table saved?
Yes. Private tables stay on the server between sessions. The DM can also download a backup file any time, which is the copy you keep yourself.
Will my players see the map before they get there?
No. With fog on, each player sees only what their tokens can see. Secret doors, hidden NPCs and GM notes never reach their screens at all.
Can I use maps I already have?
Yes. Upload the image, then draw walls and doors over it if you want fog and doors to work on it too.
Can we talk over voice?
Yes, on computers and phones. Press Join voice; mute keeps you listening. It goes straight between your browsers, so headphones and Wi-Fi work best.
